# Contributing to CSR Editor

Before opening a pull request, describe the change and confirm that you have
the right to submit all code, documentation, and other material it contains.
Identify any third-party material and its license in the pull request.

CSR Editor is offered under the AGPL-3.0-only license and through separate
commercial licenses. Before a contribution can be merged, the contributor
must complete and sign the [Contributor License Agreement](CLA.md). Email the
signed agreement to **contact@chipguys.top** and include your GitHub
username and pull request number. Do not post the signed document publicly;
it may contain personal information. A maintainer will acknowledge receipt
before merging. An employer or other rights holder must also authorize the
contribution if you do not own the rights yourself.

The CLA does not transfer copyright ownership. It gives the maintainers the
rights needed to distribute the contribution under the project's open-source
and commercial terms.
